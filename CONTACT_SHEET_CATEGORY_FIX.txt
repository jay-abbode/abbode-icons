CONTACT SHEET — WHY "HOLIDAY" STOPPED AT ~24 (and the fix)
==========================================================

WHAT WAS HAPPENING
  There's no hard cap at 24 (the count goes to 40). The model was choosing to
  stop. The system prompt frames the job as "a SMALL, tasteful set", then says
  PRECISION BEATS QUANTITY / "returning far fewer is better", and the request
  adds "fewer is completely fine". Right for vague themes — but for a broad one
  like "holiday", where 50 icons literally sit in Holiday categories, it makes
  the model stop scanning an ~800-line catalog once it has a comfortable
  handful. Hence ~24, never 50.

THE FIX (lib/contactSheet.ts)
  1. Deterministic category seeding. If the theme names a category — "holiday"
     matches Holiday / Holidays / Holiday Party / Winter Holiday — every icon in
     those categories is seeded first, exhaustively, in catalog order. That part
     no longer depends on the model at all. If the seed alone covers your count,
     the model isn't even called (faster, fully repeatable).
  2. The model fills whatever the seed doesn't — and it's asked for a deeper
     list when seeds exist so re-picked seeds don't eat slots.
  3. New prompt rule: on broad themes (a whole category, a season, a holiday)
     scan the ENTIRE catalog and include every genuine match up to the limit;
     "fewer" is only for when real matches run out. "christmas" (no category by
     that name) still goes through the model's semantic matching as before.
  Everything downstream is unchanged: color limits, palette, Allow drafts,
  exclusions, and the per-sheet budget all still apply to seeded icons.
