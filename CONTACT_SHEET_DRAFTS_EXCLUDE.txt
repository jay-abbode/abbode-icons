CONTACT SHEET — ALLOW DRAFTS + STICKY EXCLUDE / REGENERATE
==========================================================

ALLOW DRAFTS
  New "Allow drafts" checkbox in the color-limits row (next to Limit Palette).
  Off by default = active designs only (unchanged). On = DRAFT icons can be
  picked by the model AND show up in the add-by-name list.

EXCLUDE + REGENERATE
  The ✕ on an icon now does two things: removes it from the sheet AND marks it
  excluded. Excluded icons are sent with every Regenerate, filtered out of the
  pool BEFORE the model picks, so they can't come back — and the sheet refills
  to your requested count with other icons.

  A "Kept out on regenerate" strip appears under the Regenerate button listing
  the excluded icons. Click an icon's ✕ there to allow it again, or Clear to
  reset. Exclusions clear automatically when you change the theme (a new theme
  is a fresh start), and hand-adding an excluded icon by name un-excludes it.
