ABBODE ICONS - AUTO-CROP KEEPS THE PADDING UNDER PADDED LETTERS
===============================================================

WHY
  Polka Dot, Shadow and Stripe letters are padded underneath so every letter
  sits on a canvas as tall as the Q (whose tail drops below the baseline) and
  they all load at the same scale in the previewer.

  Auto-crop trimmed every pushed PNG to 5 px on all four sides, so a portal
  send would have cut that padding straight back off and reported success.

WHAT CHANGED
  A PNG that is already tight at the top and on both sides (within 5 px) is
  left alone, whatever space it has underneath. It shows up as:

      PADDING KEPT   Polka Dot A - Black and Tusk  (tight at the top and sides
                     - space underneath left in place)

  Everything else is cropped exactly as before: a fresh DesignShop export has
  padding on all four sides. Checked against all 1,385 originals in
  scripts/crop_pngs/backups: every crop decision and every output byte is the
  same as with the previous script.

  To trim the space underneath anyway:
      python scripts\crop_pngs\crop_pngs.py --only "Icon Name" --apply --trim-underneath

FILES IN THIS ZIP (extract into the repo root, overwrite)

  scripts/crop_pngs/README.md
